FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the app work offline?
Yes. The doctrine, offenses, tools and practice questions all work fully offline, with no account needed.
Which states are covered?
All 50 — with a per-state overlay for penal codes, DUI, use of force, carry, statutes of limitation and cannabis rules.

No. It is an independent educational reference. The 400+ questions are POST-style practice — not the official exam — and the app is not affiliated with any agency, POST or the DOJ.
What constitutional material is included?
4th, 5th, 6th and 14th Amendment doctrine drawn from 65 landmark U.S. Supreme Court cases.
Does it cover DUI?
Yes — DUI, SFST and DRE reference plus BAC legal limits, based on NHTSA material.
