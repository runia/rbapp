Privacy Policy

Police USA — Field Guide is designed to protect your privacy completely. It's a fully offline reference — built to help you look things up, not to learn anything about you.

We collect nothing. No account, no login, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

We collect nothing

The app does not collect, transmit or store any personal data on any server. There is no account, no login, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Everything stays on your device

Your selected state, favourites, appearance setting, and any text you enter into the narrative constructor, field-interview card or other builders are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.

Using the internet

The app works fully offline. It makes no network requests of its own — the only exception is when you tap an external “source” link, which opens an official public-domain legal website in your browser.

No advertising, no third parties

There are no ads, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs and no in-app purchases. No data is shared with anyone because none is collected.

App Store rating prompt

The app may ask Apple to show a standard App Store rating prompt after a passing practice test. This uses Apple's system mechanism and shares no information with us.

Data deletion

Because nothing leaves your device, deleting the app removes all associated data.

Children

The app is a professional reference aimed at adult officers, recruits and students, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Content & disclaimer

Facts are drawn from public-domain U.S. government material with our own plain-English summaries. This is an independent educational reference only — not legal advice, not agency policy, and not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by any law-enforcement agency, POST, the DOJ or any government body.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Contact us and we'll be glad to help.
